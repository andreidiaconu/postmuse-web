Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

PostMuse is an app for designing stories and posts for social media from templates. It is published by DIACONU MOBILE APPS SOCIETATE CU RASPUNDERE LIMITATA (“Diaconu Mobile Apps”, “we”, “us”), a company registered in Romania. On Google Play, our developer name is HeyNap.

This policy covers the PostMuse app on iOS and Android and the website postmuseapp.com. It explains what information is collected, where it goes, who processes it for us, how long it is kept, and the rights you have. We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

  • PostMuse has no accounts. Your photos, videos and designs are kept on your phone and are not uploaded to us.
  • The app sends usage data to Amplitude and Google Firebase. It includes the text you type into a design and the words you search for, so please don’t put private information in your designs’ text.
  • The app sends crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Stock photos come straight from Unsplash, which sees your searches.
  • Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we don’t show ads in the app.

1. What we collect

1.1 Your photos, videos and designs: kept on your phone

When you make a design, PostMuse uses the photos and videos you pick or take, and the text, fonts, colors and other choices you make. Your designs are saved in the app’s own storage on your phone, and videos you add are copied there too. Finished designs are saved to your photo library, copied to your clipboard, or shared to the app you choose. None of these files are uploaded to us.

The app asks for access to your camera and your photo library only so you can use your photos and videos and save your designs. Your phone’s own backups (iCloud or Google) may include the app’s data, depending on your settings.

1.2 Usage data

To understand how PostMuse is used and to improve it, the app sends usage data to Amplitude and Google Firebase Analytics:

  • How you use the app: screens opened, templates and collections picked, tools used and the values chosen (such as fonts, filters, colors and sizes), designs saved or shared, the paywall shown and purchases started, completed or restored (with the product and price), ratings and links tapped, and notifications received or opened.
  • Text: when you edit the text of a design, the old and new text; the words you search for in stock photos; the text of notifications we send you; and error messages.
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language and mobile carrier.
  • Approximate location: country, region and city, worked out from your IP address. We never collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Identifiers: random identifiers that these services create for your app installation, your purchase ID from RevenueCat (see 1.4), your push token (see 1.6), and whether you are a subscriber. On iPhone and iPad, Amplitude also uses the identifier Apple gives to apps from the same developer (IDFV). On Android, these services may use your device’s advertising ID.

We use this data to understand how the app is used and to improve it, not to build advertising profiles. We don’t ask iPhone users for permission to track them, and we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1.3 Crash reports

When the app crashes or runs into an error, it sends a report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it: the error, where in the app’s code it happened, the recent usage events described in 1.2 (which can include text you typed), device information, and the identifiers described in 1.2.

1.4 Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never receive your card details. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, receives a random purchase ID for your app installation, the products you buy, transaction identifiers and your subscription’s status, so the app knows what you have unlocked.

On iPhone and iPad, RevenueCat also collects a token from Apple’s AdServices framework once per installation, which tells us whether the install came from one of our Apple Search Ads campaigns. It doesn’t identify you, and it doesn’t need permission to track.

1.5 Stock photos

Free stock photos come from Unsplash. When you browse or search them, the app connects to Unsplash directly, so Unsplash receives your IP address and your search words, and is told when you pick a photo. Unsplash handles this under its own privacy policy.

1.6 Templates, remote settings and notifications

The app downloads our template catalog from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and our settings from Firebase Remote Config. These requests carry your app installation’s random identifier, but no information about you.

The app also registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your phone a push token. We use it to send you messages from us, such as new templates or offers. They show only if notifications are allowed on your phone, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings.

1.7 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. If you use our support form, it is run for us by Typeform, which receives what you enter. We use this only to answer you.

1.8 The website

postmuseapp.com is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads. Some posts embed YouTube videos in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode: YouTube receives your IP address when the video loads and may set cookies if you play it. Links on try.postmuseapp.com are handled by Branch, which receives your IP address and device details when you tap one, to send you to the app or the store.

1.9 What we don’t collect

PostMuse has no accounts, so it never asks for your name, email address or a password. It doesn’t access your contacts, calendar, microphone or precise location, and there are no ads in the app.

4. Who else sees your data

  • When the law requires it: we disclose data if a valid legal request obliges us to, and only what it requires.
  • If the company changes hands: if PostMuse is sold or merged, the data we hold moves with it, under this policy, and we’ll tell you before that happens.

We don’t sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

5. How long it’s kept

  • Your designs, on your phone: until you delete them or delete the app.
  • Usage data: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Installation identifiers and push tokens: until they are deleted, after which Firebase removes them within 180 days. A push token stops working when you turn notifications off or delete the app.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Emails and support messages: for as long as needed to help you, then up to 2 years.

6. Deleting your data

  • Delete any design in the app, from My designs.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that phone. Copies in your phone’s backups follow your backup settings.
  • To delete the usage data, crash reports and purchase data linked to your app installation, write to us (section 12). Because PostMuse has no accounts, we may ask for something that lets us find it, such as the order ID on your Apple or Google receipt.

7. Security

Your designs are protected by your phone’s own security (its passcode and storage encryption). Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit.

8. Children

PostMuse is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we’ll delete it.

9. Your rights

9.1 EU, EEA and UK

You have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You can also complain to a data protection authority: ours is the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), or you can contact the authority where you live.

9.2 California and other US states

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to access, correct and delete it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don’t sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

9.3 How to use them

Your designs are on your phone, where you can see, change and delete them yourself. For what we hold (usage data, crash reports and purchase records), write to us at the address in section 12. We answer within 30 days, and may ask for details that help us find your data.

10. International transfers

We are based in Romania. Firebase, Amplitude, RevenueCat and Branch process data in the United States and other countries. For those transfers, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when PostMuse changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects how your data is handled, we’ll update this policy and tell you in the app or on this website before it takes effect. Our Terms of Use cover the rest of how PostMuse works.
