Where can I find the right Valentine’s Day Photos

Our recommendation is to use a personal photo. Saying “I love you” or really anything sentimental to someone close is deeply personal. This is why the image you use should be something that the 2 of you share on a deeper level. Don’t just pick the best picture you have of the 2 of you, but pick the image that best reminds you of a special moment you shared, like you holding hands, goofing around in the elevator mirror, or maybe something fun you did together.

Whatever you do, though, nude photos are not cool for romance. It had to be said 😎

Emoji or simple Story ideas

Sometimes not a lot needs to be said. The simple fact that you send a personal message is enough for the other person to get the point. There are, however, symbols that can help you get the message across. The heart is the most obvious symbol you can use. Did you know there are 27 heart-related emojis or characters?
